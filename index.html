Privacy Policy (English)

App: Sudoku - Brain Game (Sudoku - Zeka Oyunu) · Package: com.mujde.sudokuzeka · Developer: MUJDE · Effective: 26 October 2026

In short

Sudoku - Brain Game does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The app has no accounts, sign-up, ads, analytics or third-party services. It does not request internet permission and works completely offline.

Information kept on your device

Your game progress (solved puzzles, times, records, stars, unfinished games and settings) is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else and is not included in cloud backups.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data from Settings > Apps, deletes all of this information.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used for a short vibration on taps when enabled in Settings. It has nothing to do with personal data.

Children

The app does not collect information from any user, including children.

GDPR and KVKK

Because no personal data is processed, no data is transferred or processed under the GDPR or Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK). You can still send any questions about your rights to the address below; requests are answered within 30 days.
